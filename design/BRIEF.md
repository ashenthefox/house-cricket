# House Cricket — brief

**In one sentence:** A house cricket bought as gecko food escapes the tank and explores a whole house, room by room, rescuing the crickets who scattered from its feeder cup. On the way it outwits ten big, scary-but-friendly geckos without hurting any of them.

**The feeling:** small and brave in a huge, warm, slightly spooky house. The fun of a Metroidvania, where you explore, find something new and open up a part of the map you couldn't reach before. Washed in soft watercolor. It's funny (the geckos are polite about wanting to eat you, and everything that chirps turns out not to be a cricket) and gets heartfelt at the end, when all the crickets you saved sing together.

## What they asked for

- [ ] Probably called **House Cricket** (the name is settled as House Cricket)
- [ ] The hero is a cricket **bought to feed a gecko, but it escaped**
- [ ] **An opening that shows the escape** ("we def need")
- [ ] **Metroidvania style:** moving through **the house**, goal is **rescuing your fellow crickets**
- [ ] **10 zones** ("say, 10": loose on the exact number), **each a different style**, "probably" **different rooms of the house**
- [ ] **A "Boss Gecko" fight in each zone**, each saving **a different cricket**
- [ ] **Research real crickets and match their chirps properly**
- [ ] **In a zone you hear the trapped cricket chirping, louder/softer to guide you to it**
- [ ] **Items needed to reach some areas**
- [ ] **Explore and build a map**
- [ ] **Boss fights aren't about hurting the gecko:** avoiding, stunning, distracting, "putting it to sleep or something"
- [ ] **Swap your player to any saved cricket**; "maybe" **slightly different stats** (they wrote "states"; read as stats)
- [ ] **Art leans watercolor**, all of it
- [ ] **The Gecko is scary but friendly**
- [ ] **A "Cricket Carrier" where rescued crickets live, which gets nicer as you get more**, like the castle build-out in Suikoden
- [ ] "Maybe" **other crickets/insects can join you** (loose)
- [ ] **A range of enemies**, "maybe other insects" (loose: they weren't sure)
- [ ] **A plot/joke/puzzle around a bat, a tree frog, a chimney sparrow, and other things that make cricket-like noises but aren't crickets**
- [ ] **Gamepad** (`--gamepad`): playable start to finish with a PS5 controller

## Who, where, how long

Players who like exploring, families, and anyone fond of small creatures. Desktop with keyboard or controller is the main way to play. Phones work too, held sideways with touch controls. The full game runs about 2 to 3 hours (large scope), in sittings of 15 to 30 minutes, with save points in every zone.

## Style references

- **Metroidvania (the genre):** one connected map. Found items let you move in new ways, and those moves open earlier dead ends. An auto-drawn map, save rooms, backtracking for secrets. We take the structure, not any one game's world, characters or look.
- **Suikoden's castle build-out:** in that series, your headquarters visibly grows as you recruit people, gaining shops, rooms and services, and the growth is a big reason to look for optional recruits. We take that idea for the Cricket Carrier: a pet-store plastic cricket keeper that changes from a bare box into a cozy cricket town as crickets and other friends move in. We copy none of its castles, characters or names.
- **Watercolor:** soft washes, visible paper grain, bleeding edges, ink linework only on characters, warm household light. Storybook, not photo-real.
- **Real crickets:** real species, the right chirp rhythms and pitches (researched; see the design's sound section), real behavior (only males sing, crickets hear through their front legs, geckos wag their tail tip before they strike).

## Settings and decisions made for them

- Web game for desktop and phones (landscape), with keyboard, touch and full controller support.
- Large scope, judged from the request (10 zones, 10 bosses, a growing hub, a cast and a story).
- $20 budget (the default). The design says what more budget would add.
- Plan only, shared as a page with about $5 of mock pictures.
- **2D side view**, not 3D.
- The hero is a male house cricket, since only males chirp. Its call can be answered by trapped crickets.
- "Chimney sparrow" becomes the chimney swift, the real bird that nests in chimneys and twitters. The mix-up itself becomes a running joke: she's tired of being called a sparrow *and* a cricket.
- Bosses are ten different real gecko species, each in its own room, so every fight looks and plays differently.
